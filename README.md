# The Flag Cup ⚽

A flags and World Cup quiz game for kids — play it here:
**https://jackgriffin83.github.io/flag-cup/**

- **Match of the Day** — a daily challenge: everyone in the world gets the
  same 10 questions each day. Play every day to build a 🔥 streak.
- **Flag Quiz** — 210 flags: every UN member state plus Palestine, Kosovo,
  the Vatican and all four UK home nations, across eight levels from
  Kick-Off to Penalties (the micro-states), plus a BONUS level of flags of
  former countries (the USSR, Yugoslavia, Zaire, Siam…).
- **Find the Flag** — hear a country's name read aloud, tap its flag.
- **Capitals Quiz** — see the flag, name the capital, nine levels. Countries
  with disputed or multiple capitals sit this one out honestly.
- **Footy Quiz** — six levels, ~210 questions: World Cup rookie and legend trivia (through
  2026), superstars, clubs, every nation's men's AND women's World Cup
  trophy count, and a Kit Quiz with drawn shirts (whose hoops? whose sash?).
- **Duel** — two players, one screen, sudden-death penalties if it's a draw.
- **Lightning** — 60 seconds, how many flags can you name?
- **Trophy Room** — stars per mode, best streak and lightning records, two
  printable certificates (50 ⭐ and 99 ⭐), and a printable all-flags poster.
- **Flag Book** — a sticker album of every flag with a fun fact and its
  capital, each read aloud on tap.
- **Players** — everyone plays under their own name with their own stars,
  streaks and records.

One self-contained HTML file: every flag is generated SVG (including the full
crests — Spain's quartered arms, Mexico's eagle, Croatia's chequy and crown,
Brazil's 27 stars and motto), sounds are synthesized WebAudio, reading aloud
is the browser's speech synthesis, and progress lives in localStorage.
No build step, no dependencies, works offline — download `index.html` and
open it anywhere.

Wrong answers never punish: the button wobbles, the game says "good try!",
and the player keeps trying. Stars come from first-try answers — and flags
you miss quietly come back more often until you know them.
